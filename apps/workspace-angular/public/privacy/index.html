What CommandCore does

CommandCore connects authenticated AI clients with computers enrolled by their operators. The server manages account-to-device authorization and sends requests over authenticated device connections.

Categories to review in the final notice

  • Account identifiers and OAuth authentication information provided by the configured identity provider.
  • Device registration, online status, capability and permission metadata.
  • Operation, activity and security-audit metadata needed to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service.
  • Optional managed Browser session metadata, subject to its account-bound ownership and consent mechanisms.

Hosted versus self-hosted deployments

A self-hosted installation is administered by its own operator; the published source code does not establish the privacy practices of every independent deployment. For the hosted service, the responsible operator and applicable service providers must be identified in the reviewed publication copy.

Retention, rights, and contact

Publication blocker: exact retention/deletion procedures, data subject requests, transfer details and a verified operator contact have not been approved for this draft. Do not interpret this draft as a final description of those practices.
